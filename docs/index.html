03 / THE METHOD

Local by design.
Honest by default.

Use what machines are good at—text-to-text summarization and consistent math. Keep payment and eligibility verification in human hands.

01

The text stays here.

The brief is processed in JavaScript and a Web Worker. Model inference runs locally in your browser after downloading openly available weights. No account, form submission or hosted prompt API.

02

Math is fully visible.

One honest formula: (reward × (1 − fee) × your odds − costs) ÷ hours. Never replace assumptions with “AI confidence” or call a posted bounty an actual receipt.

03

Proof is the deliverable.

Checklists are generated from explicit phrases like “repo,” “video” or “test.” The open model adds a draft synthesis; you must verify it against actual task requirements.
